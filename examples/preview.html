Floorplan 3D Card — preview without Home Assistant

Serve the repo root (python3 -m http.server) and open /examples/preview.html. Query: ?room=living, ?popup=light.living_room, ?theme=light, ?rotate=1, ?height=700px, ?bare=1. Icons come from @mdi/js on a CDN (inside Home Assistant the card uses HA's own).
